# 3.2 Analyze logs with Loop [UI]

Loop is Braintrust's AI assistant for your data. Use it to investigate the seeded
logs.

## Task

In the `sales-assistant` project, open Loop and ask it to do three things:

1. Surface interesting logs for you to look at.
2. Given a specific trace id, find similar logs.
3. Identify themes in the potential failure modes of the agent.
